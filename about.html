About Duskborne

Hi everyone, I am Arhaan, widely known in the digital space by my code name Duskborne. I am a passionate and driven Web Developer and Content Creator who loves bringing ideas to life on the internet. Whether I am crafting semantic layouts or diving into creative script writing, I am constantly exploring the intersection where technology meets compelling storytelling.

For me, development isn't just about writing code that works; it's about building immersive experiences that look incredible and function seamlessly. Operating under the banner of Duskborne, I treat every project as a new chapter in my continuous journey of learning and digital creation.

My Growth & Background

Journey & Academics

My journey into the world of technology started roughly two years ago while I was studying in the 9th grade. What began as simple curiosity quickly evolved into a dedicated passion. Looking back at my progression from building basic semantic structures with HTML to designing responsive interfaces in CSS and injecting dynamic life through JavaScript, every step has shaped how I solve problems today.

Balancing my high school academics—where my favorite subject happens to be Physics—with my deep-dive into software development has taught me discipline and analytical thinking. Physics gives me a framework for understanding how things work fundamentally, while coding gives me the canvas to build them myself. Even though my long-term career path is still open and flexible, my direction in tech and creative media is clearer than ever.
